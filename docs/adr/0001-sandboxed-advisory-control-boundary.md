# ADR 0001: Sandboxed Advisory-Only Architecture & Zero Physical Actuation Boundary

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Industrial Control Systems (ICS), Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs), and Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) networks manage critical physical assets in municipal water treatment and distribution infrastructure. Cyber incidents in these environments (e.g., False Data Injection Attacks, Stuxnet-like setpoint tampering, or sensor spoofing) can cause catastrophic physical damage (over-pressurization pipe bursts, contamination from backflow, or tank run-dry scenarios).

A major danger of automated "cyber defense" systems in operational technology (OT) is inadvertent unsafe actuation: if an automated AI system issues control commands (e.g., closing valves, starting pumps, or changing chemical dosing) based on flawed state estimates or compromised feedback loops, the defense system itself becomes an attack vector.

## Decision
We enforce a **strict, non-negotiable architectural boundary**:
1. **Advisory Only**: PROJECT LASTLINE is strictly an advisory, situational-awareness, and decision-support platform.
2. **Zero Physical Actuation Interfaces**: The backend codebase and runtime environment shall contain **zero write-capable interfaces** to industrial control hardware. There are no Modbus write function codes, DNP3 control commands, CIP setpoint write handlers, or physical actuator actuation endpoints.
3. **Twin Simulation Before Presentation**: All candidate emergency responses and fallback procedures generated by the response agents are simulated exclusively in the sandboxed EPANET/WNTR digital twin before being shown to human operators.
4. **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Requirement**: Every operational recommendation requires explicit human verification, approval, and manual out-of-band execution by qualified plant operators.
5. **Architectural Safety Tests**: Continuous automated regression tests scan the API endpoints, route definitions, and service interfaces to verify and prove that no write-capable control paths exist.

## Consequences
- **Positive**: Eliminates the catastrophic risk of unauthorized or AI-hallucinated physical actuation of critical water infrastructure.
- **Positive**: Complies with NIST SP 800-82 (Guide to Operational Technology Security) and ISA/IEC 62443 standards.
- **Trade-off**: Requires human operator presence to manually execute verified fallback procedures on physical switchgear or approved SCADA consoles.
